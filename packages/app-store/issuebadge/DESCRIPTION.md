IssueBadge turns every Cal.com booking into a verifiable digital certificate. When a
meeting, class, workshop or training session ends, each attendee automatically receives a
branded certificate of attendance or completion by email — with a public verification
page, a QR code and one-click sharing to LinkedIn.

Ideal for coaches, tutors, trainers, consultants, fitness and yoga instructors, and anyone
running CPD or compliance sessions who wants proof of attendance without manual work.

**What you get**

- **Automatic issuing.** Certificates go out the moment a Cal.com meeting ends (or as soon as
  a booking is confirmed — your choice). No spreadsheets, no exports.
- **One certificate per event type.** Map each Cal.com event type to its own certificate
  design, so a "Yoga Class" and an "Onboarding Workshop" get different credentials.
- **Verifiable by anyone.** Every certificate has a unique ID, a public verification page
  and a QR code. Recipients can add it to LinkedIn in one click.
- **No duplicates.** Re-fired webhooks and rescheduled bookings are de-duplicated by
  booking ID.
- **Your brand.** Design certificates in IssueBadge's drag-and-drop editor or pick one of
  the ready-made templates.

**How to connect**

1. In IssueBadge, create a webhook endpoint and choose the certificate to issue.
2. In Cal.com, go to Settings → Developer → Webhooks and add the IssueBadge endpoint URL
   with the **Meeting Ended** (or **Booking Created**) trigger.
3. Done — attendees receive their certificate automatically.

Get started at [issuebadge.com/h/integration/cal-com](https://issuebadge.com/h/integration/cal-com).
