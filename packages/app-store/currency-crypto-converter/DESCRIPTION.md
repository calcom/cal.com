## Currency & Crypto Converter

Convert between the world's major fiat currencies (EUR, USD, GBP, CHF) and the leading cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, USDC) in one place, with exchange rates that update live.

### Why people use it

- Live rates pulled from public exchange-rate and crypto price sources, refreshed every time you open the tool.
- Fiat and crypto in one converter, so you can instantly see what an amount is worth across both worlds.
- No account, no tracking, and no payment is ever processed. It is a reference tool, nothing is bought or sold.

### Who it is for

Freelancers, agencies and teams that invoice or get paid in crypto, and anyone who wants a fast, reliable check on what a price means in Bitcoin, Ether or a stablecoin.

Built and maintained by the team at kryptoboersen.net, an independent German comparison site for crypto exchanges.
