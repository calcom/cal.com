Proton Calendar is the end-to-end encrypted calendar from Proton AG. It keeps your event titles, descriptions, locations, and participants private through zero-access encryption.

Cal.diy syncs availability and creates events through CalDAV via [Proton Bridge](https://proton.me/mail/bridge). After installing Proton Bridge, enable CalDAV access for your Proton Calendar and use the local CalDAV URL it exposes along with the Bridge-generated app password to connect this integration.
