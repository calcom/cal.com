Clara is the website conversion agent that turns your visitors into booked Cal.com
meetings — and it doesn't just type. Clara talks.

Add Clara to your website — it's one line of code. Connect your Cal.com account, and
Clara greets every visitor, answers their questions out loud in their own language from
your own site content, handles objections, and books the right meeting at your real, live
Cal.com times — without the visitor ever leaving the page.

**Clara speaks to your visitors.** A real voice on your site: Clara answers out loud, in
the visitor's own language, with native voices in ten languages — American English,
British English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Portuguese, Hindi, Japanese, and Chinese.

**What you get**

- **A real voice, not just a text box.** Clara answers out loud in native voices,
  automatically matched to the visitor's language.
- **Books real Cal.com times in chat.** Clara pulls live availability from your event type
  and books it inside the conversation — no calendar-tetris, no bounce to a separate page.
- **No bot to build or train.** Clara reads your website automatically and answers
  accurately out of the box.
- **Never books a bad slot.** Every time is re-verified against Cal.com the moment before
  booking, and Clara only uses the contact details the visitor actually typed.
- **Qualifies, then books.** Clara handles questions and objections first, so the meetings
  that land on your calendar are ready to show up.

**How to connect**

1. Add Clara to your website — one line of code.
2. Paste your Cal.com API key and pick the meeting type you want Clara to book.
3. Clara starts turning visitors into confirmed Cal.com bookings.

Get started at [claraconverts.com/guide/cal-com](https://claraconverts.com/guide/cal-com).
