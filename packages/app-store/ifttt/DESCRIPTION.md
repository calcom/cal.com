Automate, save time, and get more done with Cal.com and IFTTT. Easily connect Cal.com to 1000+ apps and devices to create time-saving workflows - no code required.
<br/>
## **How IFTTT works**
IFTTT links two or more apps with automations called Applets. Choose a trigger, pick an action, and customize it to your needs. You can enable 100,000s of ready-made Applets or create your own in minutes.
<br/>
## **Key features**
- Connect Cal.com to 1000+ apps, services, and devices: Integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, task management, calendar, and communication platforms with Cal.com triggers and actions.
- Use IFTTT on the go: Our popular mobile apps for iOS and Android allow you to connect Cal.com to widgets, custom notifications to your phone, and location-based triggers.
- Increase your productivity: Automations are easy to set up, personalize, and you can get started with your first Applet in minutes.
<br/>
<br/>
## **Get started**
- Click **Visit** to access the Cal.com service page on IFTTT or go to https://ifttt.com/cal_com
- Click **Connect Cal.com** to connect your account to IFTTT
- Click and enable any of the published Applets available or click **[Create](https://ifttt.com/create)** to easily build your own
