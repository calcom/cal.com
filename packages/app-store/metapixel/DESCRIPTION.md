> ⚠️ **HIPAA & Healthcare Compliance Notice:**
> Meta does not execute HIPAA Business Associate Agreements (BAAs). Under US Federal Trade Commission (FTC) enforcement and HHS OCR regulations (45 CFR § 164.502), installing commercial ad-tracking pixels on telehealth, therapy, or medical appointment pages without a BAA constitutes an unlawful disclosure of Protected Health Information (PHI). When the `baa-for-hipaa` app is enabled on the event type, Meta Pixel is automatically suppressed to maintain compliance.
