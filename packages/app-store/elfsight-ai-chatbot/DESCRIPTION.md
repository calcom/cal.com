# AI Chatbot Widget for Any Website

Add a smart AI chatbot to any website in minutes — no code needed. Elfsight AI Chatbot handles customer support, captures leads, and books Cal.diy meetings right inside the conversation.

{DESCRIPTION}

### How It Works with Cal.diy

Elfsight AI Chatbot uses the Booking skill to embed your Cal.diy scheduling page directly in the chat window. When a visitor asks to book a call, demo, or consultation, the chatbot opens your Cal.diy calendar — the visitor picks a time slot without ever leaving the chat.

### Key Features

- **Works on any website** — WordPress, Shopify, Wix, Webflow, Squarespace, plain HTML
- **AI-powered conversations** — trained on your content, answers in 80+ languages
- **Built-in lead capture** — collects name, email, phone inside the chat flow
- **No-code setup** — paste one embed snippet, the chatbot builds itself from your site
- **24/7 availability** — handles inquiries, qualifies leads, and books meetings around the clock
