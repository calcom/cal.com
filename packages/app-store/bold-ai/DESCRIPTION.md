{DESCRIPTION}

Bold AI is an AI customer-communication platform that connects directly with your Cal.com account. It allows AI agents to check live availability, book, reschedule, and cancel appointments directly inside customer conversations across phone calls, voice notes, SMS, WhatsApp, web chat, email, Telegram, Facebook, and Instagram.

### Integrating Cal.com with Bold AI

1. Connect your Cal.com account securely using OAuth ("Continue with Cal.com").
2. Set up the Bold AI agent to check live calendar availability.
3. The AI agent can now handle scheduling, rescheduling, and cancellation directly inside customer conversations without any link hand-offs.
