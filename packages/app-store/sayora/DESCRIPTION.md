Sayora is the AI receptionist for UK small businesses. When you can't pick up, Sayora answers with a real, warm British voice, captures the caller's details, answers questions about your business, and books the caller straight into your Cal.com during the call.

- Books, reschedules and cancels Cal.com appointments while the caller is on the line
- Picks the right event type for what the caller asks for
- Texts you a lead summary the moment the call ends
- Filters spam and cold sales calls
- Dedicated UK phone number, unlimited minutes, one plan
