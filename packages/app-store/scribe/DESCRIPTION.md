Scribe is an email signature automation platform for companies. Connect your Cal.com organization or team to Scribe once, and every teammate's email signature automatically carries their own Cal.com booking links — turning every email your company sends into a scheduling opportunity.

- Syncs each teammate's personal booking links into their signature automatically
- One admin connects for the whole team — no action needed from teammates
- Booking links stay up to date as event types change
- Read-only access: Scribe never reads your bookings, attendees, or availability
- Works with Gmail, Outlook, and every major email client
